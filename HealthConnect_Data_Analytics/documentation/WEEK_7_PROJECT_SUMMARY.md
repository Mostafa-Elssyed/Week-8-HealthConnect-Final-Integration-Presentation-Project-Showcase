### 🤝 3. Cross-Track Collaborative Optimization Loop
To prevent data-alignment breakdown when entering the final presentation phase next week, an explicit analytical loop was established between **Data Analytics** outputs and downstream **Data Science / ML Engineering** classification scripts:

\[\text{Analytical Metrics Verification (app.py)} \longrightarrow \text{Discrepancy Caught (2.4\% High Lead-Time Skew)} \longrightarrow \text{Feature Precision Scaling Added} \longrightarrow \text{Discrepancy Vector Optimized to 1.34\% (PASS)}\]

* **The Inter-Track Bottleneck:** Long-range bookings (LeadTimes >14 days) initially caused predictive false-positives to surge within the ML pipeline due to missing feature scaling variables.
* **The Refinement Fix:** Provided stratified analytics rate tables to the ML track to balance features. A complete system re-test confirmed cross-track prediction variances dropped below **1.34%**, securing full pipeline readiness for Week 8 deployment.
